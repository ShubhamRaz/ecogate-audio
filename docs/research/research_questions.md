# Research Questions (`docs/research/research_questions.md`)

1. **RQ1 (Data Integrity & Source-Robust Partitioning)**: How do cross-split duplicate waveforms, missing annotation references, and third-party environmental noise provenance ([Piczak, 2015](https://github.com/karolpiczak/esc-50)) affect apparent classification and open-set performance when comparing random clip splitting against duplicate-safe and session-grouped splitting?
2. **RQ2 (Learned Benefit Estimation vs. Uncertainty Heuristics)**: Under group-safe evaluation and matched expert invocation budgets, does an out-of-fold trained estimator of expected loss reduction $\hat{\Delta}(z) = \mathbb{E}[\mathcal{L}(f_s(x), y) - \mathcal{L}(f_e(x), y) \mid z]$ achieve lower decision loss than ordinary fixed-threshold routing on Maximum Softmax Probability (MSP), Shannon entropy, top-2 margin, or prototype distance?
3. **RQ3 (Open-Set Disentanglement)**: Can the selective cascade reliably separate unfamiliar bird vocalizations (`bird_unfamiliar`) from non-bird environmental noise (`nonbird`) and low-quality recordings (`unresolved`) without forcing unknown bird calls into one of the four focal known species?
4. **RQ4 (Edge Quantization & Resource Trade-offs)**: How does dynamic INT8 weight quantization with post-quantization temperature recalibration ([Guo et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html)) affect student latency, memory footprint, calibration error, and routing stability across CPU and constrained edge profiles?
