# Limitations and Threats to Validity (`docs/research/limitations.md`)

1. **Incomplete Recording-Session Metadata in Public Archive**: While exact duplicates, decoded waveform matches, and ESC-50 source clip IDs are deterministically grouped, arbitrary field filenames in the Mendeley archive do not always encode verified GPS coordinates or AudioMoth deployment dates. Consequently, splits are accurately described as **duplicate-safe and provenance-grouped** rather than guaranteed geographically disjoint.
2. **BirdNET-Assisted Annotation Noise**: The underlying dataset annotations were generated with BirdNET assistance ([Kahl et al., 2021](https://colab.ws/articles/10.1016%2Fj.ecoinf.2021.101236)). Any systematic teacher bias in BirdNET labels is inherited by supervised models trained on those labels.
3. **Downstream Open-Set Scope**: Leave-one-species-out rotations evaluate downstream open-set rejection; when using externally pretrained encoders, prior exposure during upstream pretraining is not controlled. Furthermore, `other_birds` is a heterogeneous catch-all category rather than a taxonomically curated unseen species list.
4. **Physical Electrical Power Measurement**: In software-only container environments without a physical inline USB/DC power meter attached to the target device, electrical energy in Joules is explicitly marked as `not_measured`, and efficiency comparisons are reported in wall-clock latency, MACs/FLOPs, and relative compute cost units.
5. **Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W Constraints**: While the INT8 quantized student model ($<20\text{ KB}$ weights) executes comfortably within the 512 MB RAM budget of a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, invoking a large transformer expert on-device is not guaranteed to be real-time.
