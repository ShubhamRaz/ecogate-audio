# Research Gap (`docs/research/research_gap.md`)

1. **Confidence-Only Routing Fails on Non-Bird and Corrupt Clips**: Standard selective classification ([Geifman & El-Yaniv, 2017](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/7073-selective-classification-for-deep-neural-networks)) and cascade routing invoke the expert whenever student softmax confidence is low. In outdoor acoustic monitoring, low student confidence over the four known bird species frequently occurs on pure wind/rain noise or severely clipped audio—cases where invoking a costly bird-species expert yields zero decision benefit and wastes compute budget.
2. **Conflation of Unfamiliar Birds and Environmental Noise**: Prior agricultural bird detection pipelines often fold unknown birds into background noise or force closed-set predictions over a small focal species list.
3. **Uncontrolled Data Contamination in Public Acoustic Benchmarks**: Preliminary archive audits of the [Smart Rice Farming Bird Dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/p6m5tmr477) revealed cross-split duplicate groups, missing referenced paths, and ESC-50 filename matches (`2,000` noise clips matching the [ESC-50](https://github.com/karolpiczak/esc-50) naming convention). Evaluating routing algorithms without duplicate-safe group splitting risks severe optimism bias.
